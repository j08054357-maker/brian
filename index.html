Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Can't find what you're looking for? Contact our support team →

How does the 30-day free trial work?

Sign up and get full access to all features for 30 days — no credit card required. At the end of your trial, choose a plan to continue. If you don't subscribe, your data is saved for 12 months in case you return.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately with prorated billing. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle. No penalties or hidden fees.

Is my financial data secure?

Absolutely. We use 256-bit SSL encryption (bank-level), SOC 2 Type II certification, multi-factor authentication, and automatic backups. Your data never leaves our SOC 2 certified data centers. We're also GDPR and CCPA compliant.

What banks can I connect?

QuickBooks connects to 11,000+ financial institutions across the US and Canada, including Chase, Bank of America, Wells Fargo, Citi, Capital One, and most regional banks and credit unions. View the full list →

Do you offer phone support?

Phone support is included with Plus and Advanced plans. Essentials and Simple Start get 24/7 chat and email support with typical response times under 5 minutes. All plans include access to our help center, video tutorials, and community forum.

Can my accountant access my books?

Yes! Every plan includes free accountant access. Invite your accountant or bookkeeper with their own login — they get a dedicated portal with client dashboard, batch transaction review, and tax-ready reports. No extra cost.
